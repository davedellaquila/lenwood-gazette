🎄 Christmas Events 2026

The dated holiday happenings in San Jose & the Santa Clara Valley · researched and checked November 22, 2026

The events companion to our Christmas Lights Guide. That guide is where to see the lights; this one is where to go — tree lightings and parades, two different San Jose Nutcrackers, concerts, markets, Santa and the holiday trains, and the rinks. Valley first, wider Bay Area where it’s worth the drive. Every event was checked against its organizer, venue, or city on Nov 22, 2026; anything we couldn’t confirm is flagged in place, and sold-out favorites are marked so you don’t drive for nothing. Dates and prices can change — the official page always wins.

🎄 Tree Lightings & Parades

Christmas in the Park — Opening Night — Plaza de San José, downtown San Jose

Fri Nov 27 · tree lighting 6 PM · FREE

The season opens downtown: the 60-foot Community Giving Tree lights at 6 PM, and the park runs through Jan 3. Santa takes free photos (your camera) Nov 28–Dec 24. The displays themselves are in our Christmas Lights Guide.

Saratoga Tree Lighting & Candy Cane Village — Blaney Plaza, Historic Saratoga Village, Big Basin Way, Saratoga

Fri Nov 27 · Candy Cane Village 3–5 PM · lighting 5:30–6:30 PM · FREE

Free Santa photos, carriage rides and kids’ crafts in the Village first, then the city tree lights in Blaney Plaza. The Chamber’s ticketed Holiday Wine Stroll follows in the Village that evening.

Niles Festival of Lights Parade & Tree Lighting — Niles Blvd & Niles Town Plaza, Fremont

Fri Nov 27 · evening, about 6:30 PM · FREE

The East Bay’s big parade night in the old railroad district of Niles. Listings vary on the exact step-off (6 or 6:30 PM) — check the parade site before you go.

Los Altos Festival of Lights Parade — 50th — downtown Los Altos, from 1st & State Sts

Sun Nov 29 · 6–8 PM · FREE

Fifty years of illuminated floats rolling through downtown Los Altos. No rain date — a heavy-rain call is made by noon on parade day. The parade association’s site is losaltosparade.org.

Santa Clara Tree Lighting — Central Park, 909 Kiely Blvd, Santa Clara

Fri Dec 4 · 5:30–8 PM · FREE

The 40-foot tree lights with live entertainment, a holiday market, Santa photos, food trucks, and free rides on the all-electric train around the lake.

Milpitas Tree Lighting Ceremony — Milpitas Community Center, 457 E Calaveras Blvd, Milpitas

Fri Dec 4 · 7–9 PM · FREE

Santa arrives to lead the countdown for the 30-foot tree, with the Milpitas Community Concert Band, local performers, hot cocoa and Santa photos. Listed in the city’s Fall 2026 Activity Guide (link above). The same evening at the Community Center: Milk & Cookies with Santa (ticketed sessions).

Los Gatos Tree Lighting — Town Plaza Park, downtown Los Gatos

Fri Dec 4 · 5–7 PM, tree lights at 6 PM · FREE

The plaza tree lights the night before the parade — come back Saturday morning for the parade itself (below).

Cupertino Tree Lighting — Cupertino

Fri Dec 4 · 6 PM · FREE

Cupertino’s lighting is listed for Friday evening, Dec 4 — a single listing at press time, so check the city’s calendar before you drive.

Sunnyvale Holiday Tree Lighting — downtown Sunnyvale, Murphy Ave & Redwood Square

Sat Dec 5 · 5:30–7 PM · FREE

Downtown Sunnyvale lights its tree on Murphy Avenue — the same square where the Cityline ice rink runs all season (see Rinks below). Details on the city’s Winter Holiday Events page.

Los Gatos Children’s Christmas / Holidays Parade — 70th Annual — N Santa Cruz Ave & Main St, Los Gatos

Sat Dec 5 · 11 AM · FREE · rain or shine

Seventy years of kids, bands, and floats: the route runs from Almendra Ave down N Santa Cruz Ave and east on Main St to Church St. Streets close early — arrive early. (Link is LGS Recreation’s official parade FAQ.)

Campbell Carol of Lights — downtown Campbell

Sat Dec 5 · 4–7 PM · FREE

Downtown Campbell’s snow-play, petting-zoo and Santa festival along Campbell Ave — the valley’s coziest small-town kickoff. Listings at downtowncampbell.com.

Gilroy Holiday Festival & Parade — downtown Gilroy, Monterey St

Sat Dec 5 · festival from 3 PM · parade 5 PM · tree lighting 6:30 PM · FREE

An afternoon festival (Santa’s Village, Trail of Treats), then the parade at 5 and the tree at 6:30 — a full small-city Christmas in one evening.

Morgan Hill Holiday Lights Parade & Tree Lighting — Monterey Rd, Main Ave to Dunne Ave, Morgan Hill

Sat Dec 5 · parade 5:30–6:30 PM · tree lighting about 6:30 PM · FREE

The Kiwanis parade rolls down Monterey Rd and the tree lights right after at the Community & Cultural Center, 17000 Monterey Rd. Roads close from 1 PM; rain-or-shine unless heavy rain. Pair it with the Holiday Maker’s Market at Cura Contemporary (4–8 PM, see Markets).

🩰 Nutcracker & Holiday Theater

Two San Jose Nutcrackers — don’t mix them up. The city has two, at two different theaters, a week apart: San Jose Dance Theatre’s at the Center for the Performing Arts (Dec 12–13) and the New Ballet’s with Symphony San Jose at the California Theatre (Dec 19–27). Both are below.

The Original San Jose Nutcracker — San Jose Dance Theatre — Center for the Performing Arts, 255 S Almaden Blvd, San Jose

Sat Dec 12 & Sun Dec 13 · matinee & evening both days · about $60–$130 with fees on the organizer’s Eventbrite

The 61st annual production — San Jose’s longest-running Nutcracker, with live orchestra. An abbreviated Once Upon A Nutcracker for young children is listed for Fri Dec 11. sjnutcracker.com has the full grid.

The San Jose Nutcracker — New Ballet with Symphony San Jose — California Theatre, 345 S 1st St, San Jose

Dec 19–27 · from $35 · advance tickets

The Nutcracker re-set in turn-of-the-century San Jose, Light Tower and all — the New Ballet’s 10th anniversary production. For the youngest: My Very First Nutcracker, a one-act 55-minute version at 11 AM on Dec 20, 22, 23 & 27.

Hip Hop Nutcracker — Broadway San Jose — Center for the Performing Arts, 255 S Almaden Blvd, San Jose

Wed Dec 2 & Thu Dec 3 · 7 PM

Tchaikovsky remixed — a contemporary dance take on the score with MC Kurtis Blow. Tickets via Broadway San Jose / Ticketmaster.

A Magical Cirque Christmas — Broadway San Jose — Center for the Performing Arts, 255 S Almaden Blvd, San Jose

Wed Dec 16 & Thu Dec 17 · 7 PM

San Jose’s holiday cirque this year is here, not at SAP Center — a variety cirque in Christmas dress. Also at the CPA from Broadway San Jose: Love Actually In Concert (the film with live orchestra), Sat Dec 19, 7 PM, and The Carpenters Songbook: At Christmas, Fri Nov 27.

Grand Kyiv Ballet — The Nutcracker: Symphony of Lights — Hammer Theatre Center, 101 Paseo de San Antonio, San Jose

Thu Dec 10 · 7 PM only · $40–$112 (producer’s range)

One night only: the Kyiv company’s Nutcracker in illuminated costumes. Tickets through the Hammer’s box office link above.

Smuin — The Christmas Ballet — Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts, 500 Castro St, Mountain View

Dec 3–6 · evenings 7:30 PM, matinees Dec 5 & 6 · $25–$120

Smuin’s classical-meets-pop holiday ballet, in Mountain View before its San Francisco run.

Western Ballet — The Nutcracker — Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts, 500 Castro St, Mountain View

Dec 11–13 · about $45–$55

Western Ballet’s 50th anniversary Nutcracker. Sunday times and student/senior prices differ slightly between the company’s site and its poster — check the MVCPA listing before you buy.

Come From Away — Children’s Musical Theater San Jose — Montgomery Theater, 271 S Market St, San Jose

Dec 4–13 · matinees & evenings

CMT’s holiday-slot show is the 9/11-week musical, not a Christmas title — and it’s a strong one. The company’s own ticket prices weren’t posted in the listings we checked (resale prices are not box office) — cmtsj.org has them.

The 1940s Radio Hour — City Lights Theater — City Lights Theater, 529 S Second St, San Jose

Nov 19–Dec 20 · Thu 7:30 PM, Fri–Sat 8 PM, Sun 2 PM · $33–$75

A holiday musical comedy set at a live radio broadcast on Dec 21, 1942 — big-band standards and backstage chaos. Dark Thanksgiving weekend’s Sunday; see the company’s calendar.

Ren’s 12th Annual Winter Wonderland — City Lights — City Lights Theater, 529 S Second St, San Jose

Sun Dec 6 · 7 PM · $47 / $40 / $35

Jazz singer Ren Geisick’s holiday concert — the company says it always sells out, with a waitlist at the door.

Clue — San Jose Stage Company — The Stage, 490 S 1st St, San Jose

Nov 18–Dec 20

Billed “a killer comedy for the holidays” — the board game as a stage farce, running all season at The Stage.

A Christmas Carol — Silicon Valley Shakespeare — Montalvo Arts Center, 15400 Montalvo Rd, Saratoga

Dec 4–20 · MOSTLY SOLD OUT

A roving production — the audience walks with the scenes (about 1,000 steps, rain or shine, 60 seats a night). As of mid-November only a few performances had tickets left, including the Dec 12 Fezziwig’s Yuletide benefit ($200) and matinees Dec 13–14. Check Silicon Valley Shakespeare’s site for returns.

🎶 Holiday Concerts

Holiday Spectacular — Symphony San Jose — California Theatre, 345 S 1st St, San Jose

Sat Dec 12 · 2:30 & 7:30 PM · Sun Dec 13 · 2:30 PM · from $35

The Symphony’s big Christmas program — chorale, Cantabile Youth Singers, dancers from the New Ballet, a sing-along and the “Twelve Days of Silicon Valley.” Free Santa and gingerbread festivities start an hour before each performance.

Merry Mozart — Symphony San Jose — California Theatre, 345 S 1st St, San Jose

Sat Dec 5 · 7:30 PM · Sun Dec 6 · 2:30 PM · from $35

Wagner’s Siegfried Idyll (first performed on Christmas morning), Respighi, and Mozart’s 39th — with carolers in the lobby.

Season of Hope — San Jose Symphonic Choir — Cathedral Basilica of St. Joseph, 80 S Market St, San Jose

Mon Dec 14 · 7:30 PM · FREE

A free Christmas concert in the Cathedral, benefiting Catholic Charities’ Cathedral Social Ministries. In past years Season of Hope ran several nights; for 2026 the Diocese lists this one concert so far — if more nights are announced, we’ll update this guide.

27th Annual Festival of Lights — Santa Clara University — Mission Santa Clara de Asís, 500 El Camino Real, Santa Clara

Fri Dec 4 & Sat Dec 5 · 7:30 PM · $28 Fri / $34 Sat (adult) · SCU students free

The SCU Chamber Singers and Concert Choir by candlelight in the Mission church — one of the valley’s loveliest rooms for Christmas music.

California Philharmonic Youth Orchestra — Winter Concert — Cathedral Basilica of St. Joseph, 80 S Market St, San Jose

Fri Dec 18 · 6 PM

A youth-orchestra holiday concert in the Cathedral. Program and ticket details on the orchestra’s site.

A Chanticleer Christmas — Stanford Live — Stanford Memorial Church, Stanford

Thu Dec 10 · 7:30 PM · about $80–$106

The Grammy-winning men’s chorus in Memorial Church — worth the drive up the Peninsula. Stanford’s own free nights in the same church: the Festival of Carols & Lessons services Wed Dec 2 & Fri Dec 4 (free), and the Sing-and-Play-Along Messiah Fri Dec 11 ($27–$32) — music.stanford.edu has all three.

🎁 Markets & Fairs

SJMADE Holiday Fair — Santa Clara Convention Center, 5001 Great America Pkwy, Santa Clara

Fri Nov 27 & Sat Nov 28 · 11 AM–6 PM · FREE admission

The Bay Area’s largest winter craft fair — 350+ makers under one roof, the weekend after Thanksgiving. SJMADE’s second act, the Winter Wonder Market at South Hall downtown, runs Dec 12–13 (hours and details at sanjosemade.com).

German Holiday Market (Weihnachtsmarkt) — Mountain View City Hall Plaza, 500 Castro St, Mountain View

Sat Dec 12 · 11 AM–9 PM · FREE · rain or shine

One day only: Lebkuchen, Glühwein, sausages, German gifts, live performances and kids’ crafts, from the German International School of Silicon Valley.

The Great Dickens Christmas Fair — Cow Palace, 2600 Geneva Ave, Daly City

Weekends Nov 21–Dec 20 + Fri Nov 27 · 10 AM–6 PM · $50 adult / $30 youth · ADVANCE ONLINE TICKETS ONLY · parking $20

Worth the drive: 140,000 square feet of Victorian London — streets, shops, stages and roasted chestnuts. Tickets are date-specific and sold online only; there is no box office at the door.

West Coast Craft — Winter — Fort Mason Festival Pavilion, San Francisco

Sat Nov 21 & Sun Nov 22 · 10 AM–6 PM · FREE · ENDS TODAY

275+ makers at Fort Mason — the season’s biggest SF craft fair wraps up today, Sunday Nov 22. (Its Fort Mason Night Market returns Fri Dec 18, 5–10 PM.)

Gamble Garden Winter Festival — Elizabeth F. Gamble Garden, 1431 Waverley St, Palo Alto

Dec 3–5 · Holiday Marketplace Fri Dec 4 & Sat Dec 5, 10 AM–4 PM, $10 (kids 12 & under free)

Thirty-plus vendors, a vintage sale and the garden under lights. The opening-night party is Thu Dec 3 ($100 / $80 members) and holiday teas run Dec 4–5 ($85) — advance tickets for those.

Makers Market — Local Artist Holiday Street Fair — Santana Row, San Jose

Sat Dec 12 · 11 AM–6 PM · FREE to browse

An open-air fair of local artists down the Row — the same day as Santa Paws pet photos (see Santa & Trains).

Willow Glen Holiday Bubbly Walk — Lincoln Ave, Willow Glen, San Jose (check-in at 1245 Lincoln Ave)

Sat Dec 5 · 2–5 PM · $60 early / $65 after Nov 29 · 21+

A ticketed bubbly-tasting stroll down Lincoln Ave with a souvenir glass, rain or shine. The same association runs the Holiday Trolley through the Willow Glen lights Dec 6–17 — see the Lights Guide.

Ainsley House Holidays — Ainsley House, 300 Grant St, Campbell

Tours Nov 12–Dec 20, Thu–Sun 11 AM–3 PM · $12 / $10 / $8

The 1925 house dressed for a “Hollywood Holiday,” with a boutique in the Carriage House. Holiday teas ($70) run Dec 4–6 & 11–13 — book ahead, some seatings sell out — and evening open houses ($15) are Thu Dec 17 & Sun Dec 20, 5:30–8 PM.

Montalvo Yuletide — Montalvo Arts Center, 15400 Montalvo Rd, Saratoga

Yuletide Boutique Nov 20–22 · ENDS TODAY · FREE with advance registration

The Villa’s holiday boutique wraps up today, Sunday Nov 22 (11 AM–3 PM). Montalvo’s Carriage House also hosts holiday concerts in December — the Carpenters tribute, Irish Christmas in America, and David Benoit’s Charlie Brown Christmas among them (Dec 3, 10 & 11) — dates per night on Montalvo’s site.

Morgan Hill Holiday Maker’s Market — Cura Contemporary, 17395 Monterey Rd, Morgan Hill

Sat Dec 5 · 4–8 PM

A makers’ market timed to the parade evening — shop first, then watch the Holiday Lights Parade roll down Monterey Rd at 5:30. (Local listing; confirm hours with Visit Morgan Hill.)

Holidays at Filoli — Woodside — covered in our Christmas Lights Guide: the decorated estate runs Nov 14–Jan 10 with timed tickets (from $40 by day, $45 for Holiday Lights) that are selling fast — book well ahead.

🎅 Santa & Holiday Trains

Caltrain Holiday Train — San Jose Diridon Station, 65 Cahill Ave, San Jose

Sat Dec 5 · the $20 ride is SOLD OUT · the station party is FREE

The decorated train’s tickets went fast — but the party didn’t need one: a free station celebration with Santa runs at Diridon from 5 to 6:30 PM on Dec 5 (train arrives 5:19 PM), with a toy drive for Ronald McDonald House. And a first this year: the decorated train stays in regular service through Dec 13 at regular fares.

Train of Lights — Niles Canyon Railway — Niles Station, Fremont & Sunol Depot

Nov 20–Dec 30 · SOLD OUT for 2026

Every 2026 evening is sold out ($40 general / $70 first class was the price). The railway keeps an email list for cancellations and next year’s sale — join it at the link above if this is your tradition; tickets are non-refundable, so be wary of resale offers.

Holiday Lights Train — Roaring Camp — departs Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk, 400 Beach St, Santa Cruz

Nov 27–28, Dec 5–6 & Dec 12–23 · 5 & 6:30 PM · $58.25 / $38.11 standard · first class $74.15 / $48.71

Note the departure: the lights train leaves from the Boardwalk in Santa Cruz, not from Felton — Santa, sing-along and hot spiced cider aboard, about an hour. Roaring Camp’s Felton side runs the Holiday Tree Walk steam train to Bear Mountain on weekends, and a New Year’s Holiday Lights Train Dec 26–31. (The Chanukah Train is not running in 2026 — it returns in 2027.)

Holiday Night Train & Carousel — Billy Jones Wildcat Railroad — Oak Meadow Park, 233 Blossom Hill Rd, Los Gatos

Dec 14–23 & Dec 26–27 · 6–9 PM · train $10 · carousel $6 · combo $15

The region’s cheapest holiday train, in the park beside Vasona. Tickets are sold only at the booth that night — no reservations — and the railroad doesn’t run in the rain. One change this year: Fantasy of Lights is not visible from the train.

Santa’s Wonderland — Bass Pro Shops — Bass Pro Shops Outdoor World, San Jose

Photos Nov 8–Dec 24 · FREE 4×6 photo

The free-photo tradition: a 4×6 with Santa at no charge, with free reservations up to a week ahead (strongly recommended — walk-ins only as space allows). Christmas Tales storytime runs Tuesdays at 5 PM, and a Merry Morning Breakfast is set for Sat Dec 5 at 9 AM.

Photos with Santa — Santana Row — Oak Tree Lounge, 377 Santana Row, San Jose

Tue Dec 1, 8 & 15 · 6–8 PM · FREE

Free photos with Santa & Mrs. Claus during the Row’s Spirit of The Row evenings (live music and the snow showers). Bring the dog on Sat Dec 12 instead: Santa Paws pet photos in Park Valencia, 10 AM–2 PM, $10 donation to Humane Society Silicon Valley.

Photos with Santa — Westfield Valley Fair — 2855 Stevens Creek Blvd, San Jose / Santa Clara

From Fri Nov 20 through Dec 24

Santa’s season is on at Valley Fair; the mall hadn’t posted photo package prices in the listings we checked — see shopvalleyfair.com. The mall’s December extras include carolers (Dec 3, 11 & 17) and a menorah lighting (Dec 8).

Menorahs & Miracles — Children’s Discovery Museum — Children’s Discovery Museum, 180 Woz Way, San Jose

Sun Dec 6 · programming 10 AM–3:30 PM · museum admission $18

The museum’s Hanukkah celebration with Jewish Silicon Valley: the Yavneh choir at 11 AM, menorah lighting at noon, storytimes, dreidel games and a scavenger hunt.

Christmas in the Park Santa — free photos with Santa nightly Nov 28–Dec 24 at the park — see the Christmas Lights Guide.

⛸️ Rinks & Venue Specials

Downtown Ice — Skate Under the Palms — Circle of Palms Plaza, 120 S Market St, San Jose

⚠️ 2026–27 dates NOT yet posted by the rink

As of Nov 22, the rink’s own site still shows last season (which ran at $21 including skates) and third-party listings disagree about this year’s dates — so we’re not printing any. When the rink posts its season we’ll update this guide; check skateunderthepalms.com or @sjdowntownice.

Cityline Winter Ice Rink — Redwood Square, Murphy Ave, Sunnyvale

Nov 24–Jan 3 · $25 incl. skates · Ugly Sweater Night Wed Dec 23

The rink returns to downtown Sunnyvale for the first time since 2021, under the redwoods — 60-minute sessions, Wednesday theme nights, and an Ugly Sweater Night on Dec 23 (7:30 & 9 PM sessions). Book ahead.

Winter Lodge — 3009 Middlefield Rd, Palo Alto

Season Oct 16–Apr 11 · $20 + $8 skate rental · online advance tickets only

The beloved outdoor rink (since 1956) has published no special holiday nights — just its regular public sessions under the tree, closed Nov 26 and Dec 24–25. Tickets are online-only, up to 30 days ahead; gloves required.

Union Square Ice Rink — Union Square, 333 Post St, San Francisco

Nov 4–Jan 18 · $22 / $17 · worth the drive for its special nights

Drag on Ice Thu Dec 3 ($35), Silent Skate Thu Dec 10 ($35), Flashback Fridays, Wednesday theme skates, and the Polar Bear Skate on New Year’s Day — the most programmed rink in the Bay Area.

Holidays at the Winchester Mystery House — 525 S Winchester Blvd, San Jose

Holiday Open House select evenings Dec 12–30 · $25 / $15

Self-guided evenings in the decorated mansion (hot chocolate included; the exact night list is on the house’s booking page), plus the Yuletide Oddities Market from Nov 28 on selected days ($5, or a $25 season pass), a Victorian tea Nov 28, and free Santa photos on Sun Dec 20 with any ticket that day.

History Park Holidays — History San José — History Park, 635 Phelan Ave, San Jose

Heritage Holiday Tea Sat Dec 5 · $55 / $50 members · Children’s Heritage Holiday — see note

The tea (Dec 5, seatings 11 AM–3:30 PM, book ahead) and a gingerbread workshop (Dec 11) are set. The Children’s Heritage Holiday is listed for Sat Dec 19, 2–6 PM in History San José’s calendar — but one of the society’s own posts said Dec 12. Confirm the date at historysanjose.org before you plan around it.

Polar Play Days — The Tech Interactive — 201 S Market St, San Jose

Dec 2–Jan 3 · sock skating Dec 18–Jan 3

The Tech’s winter takeover: a sock-skating rink, cookie-cutter making, snowstorm-in-a-jar workshops and holiday laser shows (some activities are paid add-ons — reserve ahead).

🚫 Not This Year — and Sold Out

No Trans-Siberian Orchestra or Disney On Ice at SAP Center this Christmas. Readers ask every year: there is no TSO San Jose date in 2026 (the nearest found is Sacramento, Dec 2), and Disney On Ice’s SAP run is Feb 25–28, 2027 — a February show, not a Christmas one. SAP’s December is hockey, Rush (Dec 6) and Wang Leehom (Dec 19). The holiday cirque is at the CPA (see Theater above).

Sold out: the Niles Canyon Train of Lights (all 2026 evenings) and the Caltrain Holiday Train ride (Dec 5) — both covered above with what’s still open: the free Diridon station party, and the decorated Caltrain in regular service through Dec 13.

Not running: Roaring Camp’s Chanukah Train (returns 2027). And on the lights side, Blinky’s Drive-Thru is paused and Enchant Christmas did not return — see the Christmas Lights Guide.
