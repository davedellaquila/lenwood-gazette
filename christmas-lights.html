🎄 Christmas Lights 2026

Where to see the lights in San Jose & the Santa Clara Valley · researched and checked November 19, 2026

The headline this year: there is no drive-thru light show in Santa Clara County in 2026. Blinky’s Drive-Thru is paused, and Fantasy of Lights is walk-through only — and only four nights. This is the year of the stroll. Here is every good place to go, valley first, each name linked to its official page. For the season’s dated happenings — lightings, parades, Nutcrackers, concerts, markets and trains — see the companion Christmas Events Guide. Dates and prices were checked with organizers, cities, and county parks on Nov 19 and can change — the official page always wins.

The Big Ones

Christmas in the Park — Plaza de San José (the downtown plaza renamed from César Chávez Plaza this fall), Market St between San Fernando & San Carlos, San Jose

Walk-through · Nov 27–Jan 3 · Fri–Sun noon–10 PM, Mon–Thu noon–9 PM · FREE

The valley’s living room at Christmas: 40+ animated exhibits, 600+ decorated trees, and the 60-foot Community Giving Tree. Opening night is Friday, Nov 27 at 6 PM; Santa takes free photos (your camera) Nov 28–Dec 24. Open Christmas Eve and Christmas Day too.

San Jose Holiday Lights — Discovery Meadow, 330 W San Carlos St, San Jose

Walk-through · Nov 13–Jan 1 · nightly 4:30–10:30 PM · weekdays $25 / $19 child & senior · Fri–Sun $29 / $23 · 3 & under free

The new light garden by the Children’s Discovery Museum: 40+ illuminated sculptures across six themed areas. It took a short installation break Nov 17–20 and reopens for the run of the season — an easy pairing with Christmas in the Park, a few blocks away.

Fantasy of Lights — Vasona Lake County Park, 333 Blossom Hill Rd, Los Gatos

Walk-through ONLY this year · just 4 nights: Dec 5, 6, 12, 13 · 4–9 PM · $25 ages 13+, $10 ages 4–12 · ADVANCE TICKETS REQUIRED

The county’s beloved mile of lights is on foot in 2026 (rising costs ended the drive-thru format), with drone shows all four nights. Every person needs a prepaid ticket from parkhere.org — these nights sell out. No parking in the park; a free shuttle runs from Netflix’s Los Gatos campus on Albright Way.

Gilroy Gardens — North Pole Nights — 3050 Hecker Pass Hwy, Gilroy

Walk-through theme park · select dates Nov 21–Dec 31 (closed Dec 24–25) · most days 1–9 PM · $55 online, under 3 free · parking $25

A whole theme park dressed for the season: a 100-foot North Pole Light Tunnel, hundreds of thousands of lights, ice skating, and Santa. New Year’s Eve runs from 4 PM with midnight fireworks.

Winter Wonderland — Paseo de San Antonio, downtown San Jose (expands onto S Market St Dec 17)

Walk-through · Nov 27–Jan 3 · free admission, rides ticketed · unlimited ride wristband $40

The carnival half of the downtown holidays — a 60-foot Ferris wheel and carousel steps from Christmas in the Park. Do both in one trip; take VTA light rail and skip the parking hunt.

Neighborhood Magic

Willow Glen — west of Lincoln Ave, between Willow St and Minnesota Ave, San Jose

Free · walk or slow drive · nightly all December, after dark to ~10–11 PM

The valley’s most famous do-it-yourself display, going since 1950 — whole streets of “Charlie Brown” trees and decorated homes. The famous blocks: Cherry Ave, Glen Eyrie, Glen Echo, Glen Brook, Camino Ramon, Camino Pablo, Camino Ricardo, Carolyn, and Willow St itself. Best on foot; it’s a neighborhood, so sidewalks please, and drive like you live there. Want a perch instead? The Willow Glen Holiday Trolley

🚗 Driving it? Take our Willow Glen lights driving route (GPX) — a 3.9-mile loop threading every famous street (Carolyn, Glen Eyrie, the Caminos, and the full run down Cherry Ave). It’s formatted for the inRoute app: import the file and the turns are already pinned, silently, street by street. Start and finish on Lincoln Ave at Willow St.

Morgan Hill Holiday Lights — citywide, Morgan Hill

Free · self-guided · map grows through Dec 8

Morgan Hill turns its decorating contest into a self-guided tour: homes register with the city and land on the official map. Kick off at the Holiday Lights Parade down Monterey Rd on Sat Dec 5 (5:30 PM), with the tree lighting right after, around 6:30 PM. The Wine Trolley also runs family lights tours Dec 11–23.

Crippsmas Place — Nicolet Ave & Cripps Place, Centerville, Fremont

Free · drive or walk · Dec 5–31

Twenty minutes up I-880 and worth it: 80–90+ homes decked out around hand-painted cutouts, a tradition since 1967. Donations collected go to local charities.

Tree Lightings & Town Lights

Saratoga — Candy Cane Village & Blaney Plaza tree lighting, Historic Saratoga Village · Fri Nov 27 · village 3–5 PM, lighting 5:30 PM · free

Santana Row — 377 Santana Row, San Jose · tree & lights up all December · free — the 40-foot tree is lit nightly all season; Spirit of The Row brings live shows and free Santa photos Tue Dec 1, 8 & 15, 6–8 PM, and Snow Showers fall in Park Valencia Tue & Sat evenings, Dec 1–29.

Los Gatos in Lights — Town Plaza Park, Main St & Santa Cruz Ave · Fri Dec 4 · 5–7 PM, lighting at 6 PM · free — come back Saturday for the Children’s Parade, Dec 5 at 11 AM.

Campbell — Carol of Lights — Downtown Campbell, E Campbell Ave · Sat Dec 5 · 4–7 PM · free

Milpitas Tree Lighting — Civic Center Plaza, 457 E Calaveras Blvd · Fri Dec 4 · 7–9 PM · free — a 30-foot tree and the city’s official start to the season.

Santa Clara Tree Lighting — Central Park, 909 Kiely Blvd · Fri Dec 4 · 5:30–8 PM · free

Sunnyvale Tree Lighting — downtown, Murphy Ave & Redwood Square · Sat Dec 5 · from 5 PM · free — the 27th annual lighting, with a winter ice rink at Redwood Square running into the new year.

Gilroy Holiday Festival & Parade — downtown Monterey St · Sat Dec 5 · parade 5 PM, tree lighting 6:30 PM · free

Worth the Drive

Holidays at Filoli — 86 Cañada Rd, Woodside

Walk-through · Nov 14–Jan 10 · Holiday Lights 4–10 PM, from $45 · advance tickets recommended

The grand one: 350,000 lights across a great estate’s gardens, with a 210-foot light tunnel. Closed Thanksgiving and Dec 24–25.

Glowfari — Oakland Zoo, 9777 Golf Links Rd, Oakland

Walk-through · Nov 6–Jan 31 · 4:30–9 PM · advance timed tickets required, from ~$26, varies by night · parking included

Giant glowing lantern animals take over the zoo after dark. Tickets go on sale 28 days ahead and prime nights go fast.

Eucalyptus Avenue “Christmas Tree Lane” — 1900–2000 blocks of Eucalyptus Ave, San Carlos

Free · drive or walk · Dec 1–25, roughly 5–10 PM

The Peninsula’s answer to Willow Glen — two solid blocks of decorated homes. The city makes the street one-way Dec 4–28 to keep the crawl moving.

Deacon Dave’s — Hillcrest Ave, Livermore

Free · walk-through · opens Sat Nov 28 at 6:30 PM, through Jan 1 · Sun–Thu 6–9 PM, Fri–Sat 6–10 PM

One legendary house, 600,000+ lights, 43 years running. Weather can close it for a night — check before you drive.

WinterFest — Six Flags Discovery Kingdom, Vallejo

Nov 27–Jan 3

The show that used to light up Great America now lives in Vallejo — the closest full theme-park Christmas to the valley besides Gilroy Gardens.

Not This Year — Don’t Drive Out on a Stale Listing

Blinky’s Drive-Thru — PAUSED for 2026. Christmas in the Park’s organizers say they couldn’t secure a site that met their standard this year. Some travel sites are still advertising it at the Fairgrounds with last year’s dates and prices — those listings are stale.

Enchant Christmas at PayPal Park — did not return for 2026.

California’s Great America WinterFest — discontinued; the Santa Clara park’s final season is 2027 and it is dark this winter.

Still to confirm: Downtown Ice (Skate Under the Palms) at Circle of Palms — event listings report Nov 19–Jan 10, but the rink’s own site hadn’t posted 2026 dates as this guide went to press; check before you lace up. Widmer-World in Pleasanton (Chelsea Court) likewise hadn’t announced 2026 dates by press time; its usual run is Dec 1–31, 6–9 PM. We’ll update this guide as both confirm.

Desk notes. Book Fantasy of Lights first — four nights is all there is. Christmas in the Park + Winter Wonderland + San Jose Holiday Lights make one downtown evening; VTA light rail drops you in the middle of all three. In the neighborhoods, you’re a guest: park once, walk, keep voices and headlights neighborly. — The City Editor
