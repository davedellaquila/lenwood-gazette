# The Lenwood Gazette

A public hyperlocal daily newspaper for San Jose & the Santa Clara Valley — the paper's
own site (separate from the editor's buddy repo, `lenwood-gazette-buddy`).

- **Live site (once the public repo is created + Pages enabled):** https://davedellaquila.github.io/lenwood-gazette/
- **Editor:** Lenwood Gazette Buddy — "The City Editor"
- **Parent wire:** Neighborhood Buddy (collects/verifies; this paper selects, writes, publishes)
- **Sister paper:** [The Fiddletown Gazette](https://davedellaquila.github.io/fiddletown-gazette/) — fully separate paper, site, and newsroom; friendly colophon cross-link only.

## Rhythm
Morning ~7:00 AM · Midday ~12:30 PM · Evening ~5:30 PM PT, plus an hourly safety poll
with out-of-cycle banner updates for qualifying events (evacuation order/warning,
major freeway closure, severe-weather warning, flood notice).

## Standing features — Weather Events · This Day in History · Star Chart
- **Weather Events** (`#weather-events`, under the Safety Wire): weather events
  covered as news — storms, atmospheric rivers, heat/freeze, wind, air quality.
  Safety-relevant items are promoted into the Safety Wire / banner. See PUBLISHING.md step 8.
- **Star Chart** (`#sky`, sidebar): tonight's sky over the valley — evening chart,
  moon phase, planets, notable events — pitched honestly for city light pollution,
  with the Mt. Hamilton drive as the upgrade. See PUBLISHING.md step 9.

## Standing feature — This Day in History
A date-keyed sidebar section (after Weather): local San Jose / Santa Clara Valley
history first, wider California/national items only when the local well runs dry for
a date. Set by the Morning Edition; carried forward at Midday and Evening. See
PUBLISHING.md step 7.

## Privacy line — non-negotiable
Public sources and public events only. No residents' private data, no home addresses,
no private or family events, nothing from mail/calendar/private accounts. Every item
passes the pre-publish gate in PUBLISHING.md before it prints.
