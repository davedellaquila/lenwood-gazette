💿 Album of the Day — The Archive

Every daily pick from 🎵 Music Buddy since the series began on November 18, 2026 — newest first, with Music Buddy's own write-ups and Apple Music links, exactly as the Gazette prints them each morning. The list grows with every new pick. Music Buddy's own running list lives on their homepage.

Friday, Nov 27, 2026

Journey — Escape (1981)

A Friday record from Dave’s classic-rock shelf — Journey at their arena peak, with “Don’t Stop Believin’,” “Who’s Crying Now” and “Open Arms,” Steve Perry soaring over Neal Schon’s guitar and Jonathan Cain’s piano. Big melodies and bigger choruses, made to fill a room.

Thursday, Nov 26, 2026

Keola Beamer — Kolonahe: From the Gentle Wind (1999)

A Thanksgiving-morning record from Dave’s slack-key shelf — Keola Beamer’s nahenahe touch, where the spaces between the notes are as musical as the notes themselves, and George Winston sits in on “Ka Makani Ka‘ili Aloha.” Warm, unhurried guitar that will glow on the Quests before the drive south.

Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026

Elton John — Elton John (1970)

Following Dave's Nov 21 Elton listening — the original 1970 piano-first record behind the live session he loved, with “Your Song,” the gospel-tinged “Border Song” and “The Greatest Discovery.” Melody and piano up front, strings swelling behind; the Quests will love the space in it.

Tuesday, Nov 24, 2026

Slightly Stoopid — Top of the World (2012)

Back to Dave's real rotation for a Tuesday — Slightly Stoopid's reggae-rock and dub at its sunniest, with “Don't Stop,” “Way You Move” and Barrington Levy on “Ur Love.” Easy melody up top and a deep bottom end underneath; the Martin Logan sub will earn its keep.

Monday, Nov 23, 2026

Miles Davis — Kind of Blue (1959)

A Monday-morning home-base record — Bill Evans at the piano, melody and space first, with “So What” and “Blue in Green” unfolding slow and clear. Miles, Coltrane and Cannonball in no hurry at all; this is the kind of recording the Quests were made for.

Sunday, Nov 22, 2026

Vince Guaraldi Trio — Jazz Impressions of Black Orpheus (1962)

Back to home base for a Sunday morning — Guaraldi's trio takes the Black Orpheus tunes bossa-light and swinging, and “Cast Your Fate to the Wind” is melody first, pure and simple. Piano this warm and unhurried will glow on the Quests.

Saturday, Nov 21, 2026

Santana — Abraxas (1970)

A Saturday record from Dave's classic-rock shelf — “Black Magic Woman,” “Oye Como Va” and the slow burn of “Samba Pa Ti,” Carlos Santana's guitar singing over Latin percussion that will wake the Quests and the sub alike.

Friday, Nov 20, 2026

Eva Cassidy — Songbird (1998)

The quiet-evening voice Dave asked to add on Nov 7, finally front and center — “Fields of Gold,” “Autumn Leaves” and “Over the Rainbow” sung so close and clear the Quests will put her right in the room. A Friday-night record for the cabin.

Thursday, Nov 19, 2026

Bob Marley & The Wailers — Exodus (1977)

Marley at his most hopeful and resolute — “Three Little Birds,” “One Love” and “Jamming” over a rhythm section that breathes. Roots reggae that fills the cabin and lifts the morning, made for that vintage stereo.

Wednesday, Nov 18, 2026

The Dave Brubeck Quartet — Time Out (1959)

Odd time signatures that swing like they were always natural; Brubeck's piano at the center of the classic small-group sound; glows on the cabin stereo.
